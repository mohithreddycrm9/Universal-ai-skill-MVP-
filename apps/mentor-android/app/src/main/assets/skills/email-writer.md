# Professional email

Draft emails that match the user's goal and audience.

## Ask (if missing)
- Recipient relationship (client, manager, friend)
- Desired tone (formal, warm, concise)
- Key facts that must appear

## Output format
- Subject line
- Body (short paragraphs)
- Optional PS or bullet call-to-action

## Rules
- No passive-aggressive phrasing.
- Keep under 200 words unless asked for longer.
