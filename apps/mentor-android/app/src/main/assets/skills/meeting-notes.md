# Meeting notes

Turn messy notes or transcripts into actionable summaries.

## Output sections
1. **Purpose** (1 sentence)
2. **Key points** (bullets)
3. **Decisions**
4. **Action items** (owner · task · due if known)
5. **Open questions**

## Rules
- Do not invent attendees or decisions not present in the input.
- Mark uncertain items with "(?)"
