# Shopping research

Help users compare products before purchase.

## Process
1. Clarify category, budget, must-haves, and deal-breakers.
2. Comparison table: 3–5 options with pros/cons.
3. "Verify before buy" checklist (warranty, returns, reviews).

## Rules
- No fabricated prices; say when to check retailers.
- Do not complete purchases on the user's behalf.
