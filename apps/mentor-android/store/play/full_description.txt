Lumina is a personal assistant that lets you bring your own API keys and choose the model you trust—OpenAI-compatible APIs, Anthropic Claude, Google AI, Hugging Face Hub, or Ollama on your network.

WHY UNIVERSAL AI
• Connect providers with API key, Google, email, or mobile sign-in flows
• Track estimated spend with daily and weekly budgets
• Skill packs and plugins (/calc, /time, /fetch, and more)
• Popular workflows: morning brief, meal planning, writing polish, study help
• Home screen widget and app shortcuts for one-tap starters
• Optional biometric lock and encrypted credential storage

CHAT & PRODUCTIVITY
• Streaming replies with Fast, Balanced, or Deep presets
• Conversation drawer with search, pin, and rename
• Share text from other apps into the composer
• Prompt library for reusable starters

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
• No central chat server—we send requests only to providers you configure
• Chats stay on your phone unless you enable optional encrypted backup or sync
• Erase all local data anytime in Settings

REQUIREMENTS
You need API keys or tokens from your chosen providers. Local Ollama is optional.

Support contact is listed in the app Settings screen.
